Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Opportunities House (YOUR REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. We aim to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

Email alerts: your email address, your chosen state and category, and the date you subscribed.

Reports: the reason and details you type when you report a listing. We do not ask for your name.

Anonymous usage counts: when you open a listing, click Apply or Share, or search with no results, we record that event with the date and the listing or search words. We do not store your name, email or any personal identifier with these counts.

Website analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It records things like the pages you open, your approximate location, your device and browser, and how you arrived, using cookies or similar technologies. We use this information in aggregate to improve the site, not to identify you by name. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

On your device only: saved listings and your Match me answers (state, level, interests) are stored in your own browser. We do not receive them.

Why we use it

We use your email only to send you the opportunity alerts you asked for, and to run and improve the service. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Our legal basis

We email you only because you gave your consent by subscribing. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the site: a database and login provider (Supabase), website hosting (Vercel), an email delivery service (Brevo), Google Analytics for visitor statistics and Google Fonts for typefaces. They process data for us under their own terms and only to provide their service. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you unsubscribe, then delete it. Reports are kept as long as needed to review them and protect other users.

Your rights

You may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. To unsubscribe, use the link in any email. For anything else, write to YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use secure connections and access controls, and only our authorised team can see subscriber data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your own devices safe.

Children

Our alerts are intended for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to subscribe for you.

Links to other websites

Our listings link to official websites we do not control. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies before you apply.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
