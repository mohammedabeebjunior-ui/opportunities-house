About Opportunities House

Nigeria’s opportunity hub

Opportunities House exists to make it easy for Nigerians to find real jobs, scholarships, internships, fellowships, grants and training, without searching through dozens of websites or falling for scams.

What we do

Our team collects opportunities from official sources and lists them in one place, sorted by deadline, with filters for state and level. Visitors can save listings, get matched to what fits them, and receive free email alerts.

How we keep it trustworthy

We link to official sources. Every listing sends you to the organisation’s own website to apply.

We never charge applicants. Real jobs and scholarships do not ask for money. If anyone asks you to pay, it is a scam.

You can report problems. Every listing has a Report button. Reported listings are reviewed and removed when they are wrong or unsafe.
